ABOUT KARIM

I’m Karim ElGhazaly, an architect and Senior 3D Artist & AI Visualization Specialist. I combine architectural understanding, 3D production and AI-assisted workflows to create visual experiences that communicate design intent, materiality, atmosphere and story.

My work spans residential and hospitality projects, exterior and interior visualization, conceptual environments, and image-making workflows that bring ideas to life.
